How a passport works

Three moves, one balance. Nothing to redeem manually — NAS lands the moment your attendance is confirmed.

Step 01

Show up

Attend a NAS event, join a Nova Activity, or check in at a partner venue. Your member number does the rest.

Step 02

Earn NAS

Each action carries a fixed NAS value — +50 for an event, +10 for an activity, +5 for using a privilege.

Step 03

Climb & spend

Your total sets the tier. Tier perks unlock automatically, and privileges spend back the points you earned.

Tiers

Based on the NAS you earn in a rolling 12 months. Downgrades never happen mid-season.
